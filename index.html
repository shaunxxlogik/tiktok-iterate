TikTok Video Downloader

Copy URL on Tiktok

Copy the link to the TikTok video you want to download. Open TikTok, tap Share, then select Copy link.

Quick tip: Make sure you copy the video link, not the creator's profile link.

Paste your URL

Return to Tik-Iterate and paste the copied URL into the input field above. Click Download to process your link.

Quick tip: Double-check that the URL was pasted correctly before continuing.

Choose and download

Once your link has been processed, choose an available download format and save the video to your device.

Quick tip: Check your Downloads folder if you cannot find the saved video.
